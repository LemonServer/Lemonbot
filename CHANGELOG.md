# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-10

First stable release for the pinned Ubuntu desktop deployment.

- Allowlisted group genuine mentions and private text with serial navigation.
- Persistent isolated contexts, restart continuity, quote labeling and optional scoped memory.
- Durable inbox/outbox, exact new-self readback and UNKNOWN no automatic retry.
- Persistent pause, target-level unavailable-table handling, budgets and configurable limits.
- Name-based whitelist synchronization and startup availability reports.
- Pinned environment/setup helper, persistent user units, safe drain/restart/stop.
- Reorganized installation/operations/development docs, historical archive and MIT license.

Manual QR login and host recovery remain required. New-machine/account installation,
quoted-reply full E2E and beyond-window long-term recall have not completed real-account
acceptance. Third-party image/source license is not established by Lemonbot's MIT grant.
See [release evidence](docs/reference/releases/release-1.0.md).
